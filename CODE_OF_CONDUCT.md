# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and constructive in all interactions
- Welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Dismissive or demeaning comments
- Publishing others' private information without consent

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported by opening a private issue or contacting the maintainers directly. All reports will be reviewed and handled appropriately.
